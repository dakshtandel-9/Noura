Questions before you begin

A few things, gently clarified.

Is this an instant booking website?

No. Session and invitation requests are reviewed by the team. A preferred date is not a confirmed booking.

How do I request an invitation?

Complete the invitation form with your details and interests. The team will review your request and share next steps where appropriate.

Can I request a private session instead?

Yes. Use the separate private-session form and choose the experience that interests you.

Do I pay on the website?

There is no online payment step in this request flow.

When will I hear back?

The team will contact you after reviewing the request. A specific response time will be published only once it is confirmed.
