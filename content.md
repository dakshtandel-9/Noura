# Content brief / proposed copy, not approved business claims
All copy below is a draft for Sidhart’s review. It describes the requested positioning and documented activities; it does not assert a real venue, founder history, treatment outcome, customer count or staff qualification.

## Voice
Calm, clear, human, welcoming and specific. Use fewer words rather than vague luxury adjectives. Do not use pressure, “last few places”, exclusivity based on social status, transformation promises or medical claims. “By invitation” explains a review process, not a visitor’s personal worth. Do not imply that a reader’s body or appearance needs improvement.

This is an illustrative sequence for approval, not a published fixed itinerary. Do not attach times, inclusions or clinical consultation claims without real operational details.

## Invitation journey
Heading: **A thoughtful welcome, not an instant booking.**
1. Share your interest — tell the team what brings you here.
2. A personal review — your request is reviewed privately.
3. Receive next steps — approved requests are followed up by the team.
A request does not automatically approve membership or reserve a session. Preferred dates are preferences until the team confirms them.

## Care and privacy
Heading: **Care includes how we handle your details.**
Copy: Requests are intended for review by authorized team members. We ask for the information needed for that conversation, and explain the next step clearly.
Final wording must accurately reflect the implemented controls and approved privacy notice. Avoid “always protected” or “100% secure”.

## FAQs
**Is this an instant booking website?** No. Session and invitation requests are reviewed by the team. A preferred date is not a confirmed booking.
**How do I request an invitation?** Complete the invitation form with your details and interests. The team will review your request and share next steps where appropriate.
**Can I request a private session instead?** Yes. Use the separate private-session form and choose the experience that interests you.
**Do I pay on the website?** There is no online payment step in this request flow.
**When will I hear back?** The team will contact you after reviewing the request. A specific response time will be published only if the client confirms it.
